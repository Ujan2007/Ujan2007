## 📊 Data Science & Analytics

### 🥛 [Dairy Plant Production Analysis Pipeline & Live Anomaly Detection](https://github.com/Ujan2007/DairyPlant-End-to-End-Production-Data-Pipeline-Live-Monitoring-Dashboard)
> Analyses 60 days of plant production data to help the founding team understand operational performance and catch inefficiencies early. The deliverable is a live Streamlit dashboard that tracks key production KPIs, flags anomaly days, and visualises the impact of milk quality and SOP adherence on yield.


[![View Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Ujan2007/DairyPlant-End-to-End-Production-Data-Pipeline-Live-Monitoring-Dashboard)
